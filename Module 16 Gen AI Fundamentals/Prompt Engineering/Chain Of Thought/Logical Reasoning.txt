Role:
You are an AI systems analyst.

Task:
A logistics company operates five warehouses connected by multiple transportation routes. Each route has a different travel cost and delivery time.

Your objective is to determine the most efficient delivery path while satisfying the following constraints:

- Total delivery time must not exceed 10 hours.
- Transportation cost should be minimized.
- At least one regional warehouse must be included in the route.
- No warehouse may be visited more than once.

Instructions:
1. Analyze the available routes.
2. Compare all valid delivery paths.
3. Eliminate routes that violate constraints.
4. Select the optimal solution.
5. Present only the final route, total cost, total time, and a brief justification.

Expected Output

Optimal Route
Warehouse A → Warehouse C → Warehouse E

Total Cost
$4,850

Delivery Time
8.5 Hours

Status
Optimal Route Selected
